Vrinda Store Sales Analysis

Retail Sales Dashboard | Microsoft Excel | Business Insights

About the Project

This project analyzes Vrinda Store's 2022 sales data using Microsoft Excel, dashboards, Pivot Tables and charts. It explores sales trends, customer segments, order status, top-performing states and sales channels.

Key Business Insights

  • Women contribute approximately 64% of recorded sales.
  • Maharashtra is the highest-performing state by sales amount.
  • Amazon leads among the listed sales channels.
  • Order status and customer age-group analysis can guide marketing decisions.
